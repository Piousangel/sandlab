## r/WebGames
제목: One tray of sand, two water cups: a falling-sand puzzle about counting your cuts (browser, no install)

본문:
Small WebGPU falling-sand puzzle. Each level gives you one tool: dig a short line through dirt, then watch the physics.

The new level, "Twin Cups", has two sealed water cups under one long tray of sand. Sand sinks, water rises and spills into a pot. One cut only serves one cup, so a single cut stalls with half the level dry. Cuts over the middle do nothing. Two wide cuts, one per cup, clear in under 5 seconds.

Play: https://sandlab.app/play/1-10
Walkthrough (spoiler-light): https://sandlab.app/play/1-10/guide

Needs a WebGPU browser (recent Chrome/Edge). Feedback on difficulty welcome.

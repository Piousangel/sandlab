## r/WebGames
제목: A one-move sand & water puzzle where the obvious cut never works (browser, no install)

본문:
I've been building a small WebGPU falling-sand puzzle game. Each level gives you one tool — dig a line through dirt — and you watch the physics decide what happens.

The newest level, "Overflow", has a trap: the tray of sand is wide enough to open above either the water cup or the pot. Open the wrong half and nothing ever clears. Open the right half with a wide cut and you finish in ~6 seconds (3 stars).

Play: https://sandlab.app/play/1-9
Why it works (spoiler-light walkthrough): https://sandlab.app/play/1-9/guide

Needs a WebGPU browser (recent Chrome/Edge). Feedback on difficulty very welcome.
